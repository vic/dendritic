## A personal note from Victor

Hey! I'm [Victor Borja](https://github.com/vic) from Mexico. Everything I know, I learned by reading other people's code. I've been contributing to open source since 1999 — from the Rubinius VM, to the Apache Software Foundation, to asdf-vm, and now to the Nix ecosystem.

Programming is my job but also my passion — and for me, a way to contribute good things to the world. I create tools in the hope that they are useful and that others can learn from them, just as I've learned from others.

But open source has a cost. Creating and maintaining these libraries means spending hundreds of hours writing code, writing documentation, responding to issues, and providing community support — without expecting anything back.

I've been doing this since 1999 without taking a dime. But sponsorships are a way for the community to contribute back to open source. And I believe this world needs more kind actions.

**Thank you for supporting my work.** If you do sponsor, please mention what repo you liked the most — I'd love to know.

*Peace, vic*

## Other ways to help

Not ready to sponsor financially? You can still make a difference:

- **Star the repositories** — [den](https://github.com/vic/den), [flake-aspects](https://github.com/vic/flake-aspects), [flake-file](https://github.com/vic/flake-file), [import-tree](https://github.com/vic/import-tree), [dendrix](https://github.com/vic/dendrix), [denful](https://github.com/vic/denful), [dendritic-unflake](https://github.com/vic/dendritic-unflake)
- **Share the word** — blog about your Dendritic setup, mention it in discussions
- **Contribute code** — PRs, bug reports, and failing tests are all valuable
- **Help others** — answer questions in [community channels](/community/) **Perhaps this is the best way to contribute**
- **Use it** — real-world usage is the best feedback, and I'm really greatful for people telling me they like to use this stuff. I'm doing it for you.
