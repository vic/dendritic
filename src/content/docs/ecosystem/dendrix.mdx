<Aside type="tip">
[dendrix](https://github.com/vic/dendrix) is an aggregator of repositories from the community providing Dendritic Nix configurations, akin to initiatives like AUR/NUR — but for Dendritic Nix.


 dendrix will soon be renamed as <strong>`DUR`: Dendritic User Repository.</strong> to clarify its role in the ecosystem.
</Aside>

## What it does

dendrix provides a platform for the community to contribute, discover, and consume Dendritic modules hosted on user repositories:

- **Community-submitted** — modules authored and maintained by their original authors from their repositories, no barrier of entry
- **Dendrix Trees** — aspect import-trees from community repositories
- **Ecosystem index** — a catalog of all Dendritic projects and libraries

## The Vision

Just as NUR gives the Nix community a place to share packages, dendrix aims to be the place to share **composable configurations**. Instead of copying snippets from dotfiles repos, you import well-tested, parameterized aspects that work across NixOS, Darwin, and Home-Manager.

## Current Status

dendrix has the infrastructure in place — CI, templates, documentation, and the ecosystem guide. Its success depends on community participation. **This is where sponsorship makes the biggest difference**: funding dedicated time to build the community, review contributions, and create starter aspects that attract more contributors.
